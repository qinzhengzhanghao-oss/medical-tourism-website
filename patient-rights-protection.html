Core Patient Rights

📋

Informed Consent

Right to complete information about treatment options, risks, benefits, and costs before making decisions.

  • Complete treatment information
  • Risk and benefit disclosure
  • Cost transparency
  • Alternative options discussion
🔒

Privacy Protection

Right to medical record confidentiality and personal information protection according to privacy laws.

  • Medical record confidentiality
  • Personal data protection
  • Photo/video consent control
  • Information sharing control
🛡️

Safe Treatment

Right to receive treatment in safe, clean, accredited facilities with qualified medical professionals.

  • JCI-accredited hospitals
  • US-trained doctors
  • Sterilization protocols
  • Emergency preparedness
