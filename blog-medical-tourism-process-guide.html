The 7-Step Medical Tourism Process

Step 1: Free Initial Consultation

What Happens:

  • • Free online consultation (WhatsApp/Telegram/Video)
  • • Medical needs assessment
  • • Preliminary cost estimate
  • • Timeline discussion

What You Need:

  • • Basic medical information
  • • Treatment goals
  • • Budget considerations
  • • Timeline preferences

Time: 30-60 minutes | Cost: Free

Step 2: Medical Record Review & Doctor Matching

Our Process:

  • • Medical team reviews your records
  • • Match with 2-3 suitable doctors
  • • Doctor credentials verification
  • • Hospital accreditation check

Your Role:

  • • Provide medical records
  • • Doctor preference input
  • • Schedule doctor consultation
  • • Ask questions

Time: 3-5 business days | Documents: Medical records, test results

Step 3: Detailed Treatment Planning

Planning Deliverables:

  • • Complete treatment plan
  • • Detailed cost breakdown
  • • Timeline schedule
  • • Travel arrangements

Included in Plan:

  • • Medical procedures
  • • Hospital stay details
  • • Recovery timeline
  • • Travel & accommodation

Typical 8-Week Timeline

Pre-Treatment (Weeks 1-2)

Week 1: Consultation & Planning

Initial consultation, record review, treatment planning

Week 2: Travel Preparation

Visa processing, travel booking, pre-treatment instructions

Treatment & Recovery (Weeks 3-8)

Week 3: Travel & Pre-Op

Arrival in China, hospital admission, pre-operative tests

Weeks 4-5: Treatment Phase

Medical procedures, hospital recovery, initial follow-up

Weeks 6-8: Recovery & Return

Post-treatment recovery, travel home, US doctor coordination
