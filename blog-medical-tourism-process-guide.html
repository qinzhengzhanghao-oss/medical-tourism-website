⚠️ IMPORTANT RISK DISCLOSURE & SAFETY INFORMATION

Medical Tourism Risk Factors

Medical Risks

  • Complication Rates: Dental implant failure rate: 3-5% (similar to US standards)
  • Infection Risk: Less than 1% with strict sterilization protocols
  • Anesthesia Risks: Board-certified anesthesiologists monitor all procedures
  • Post-operative Complications: Swelling, bleeding, pain management protocols in place

Travel & Logistics Risks

  • Travel Delays: Flight cancellations, visa issues, weather disruptions
  • Cultural Adaptation: Language barriers, food differences, local customs
  • Emergency Response: 24/7 emergency support with English-speaking staff
  • Follow-up Care: Remote monitoring and local dentist coordination

Financial & Legal Risks

  • Cost Overruns: Fixed-price contracts with no hidden fees
  • Insurance Coverage: Verify with your insurance provider before travel
  • Legal Jurisdiction: Medical malpractice laws differ by country
  • Refund Policies: Clear cancellation and refund terms
