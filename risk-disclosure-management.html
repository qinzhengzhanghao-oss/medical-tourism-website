Risk Disclosure & Management

⚠️ Important Risk Disclosure

Medical tourism involves inherent risks. This document outlines potential risks and our risk management strategies.

⚠️

Important Risk Disclosure

Medical tourism involves inherent risks that must be carefully considered. Consult with medical professionals before making decisions.

We strongly recommend consulting with your local physician before considering medical tourism.

Medical Risks

Surgical Risks

  • ⚠️ Infection and bleeding
  • ⚠️ Anesthesia reactions
  • ⚠️ Extended recovery time

Treatment Risks

  • ⚠️ Results may vary from expectations
  • ⚠️ Need for additional procedures
  • ⚠️ Post-treatment complications

Travel Risks

Logistical Risks

  • ✈️ Flight delays and cancellations
  • 🧳 Lost luggage and documents
  • 🏨 Accommodation issues

Cultural & Legal Risks

  • 🗣️ Language barriers
  • ⚖️ Different healthcare regulations
  • 🏥 Emergency access limitations

Our Risk Management Strategies

We implement comprehensive risk management measures:

Medical Safety

  • ✓ Work only with ISO9001-certified hospitals
  • ✓ Verify doctor qualifications and experience
  • ✓ Comprehensive medical record review

Travel Safety

  • ✓ Managed travel arrangements
  • ✓ 24/7 emergency support
  • ✓ Contingency plans for disruptions

Insurance Recommendations

We strongly recommend:

🛡️

Medical Insurance

International coverage with evacuation

✈️

Travel Insurance

Trip cancellation and interruption
