Risk Disclosure & Management

Important: Medical tourism involves risks. Consult with medical professionals before making decisions.

1. Medical Risks

All medical procedures carry risks:

  • Surgical complications (infection, bleeding)
  • Anesthesia reactions
  • Extended recovery time
  • Results may vary from expectations

2. Travel Risks

International travel risks include:

  • Flight delays and cancellations
  • Language and cultural barriers
  • Different healthcare regulations
  • Emergency access limitations

3. Our Safety Measures

  • Work only with ISO9001-certified hospitals
  • Verify doctor qualifications and experience
  • Comprehensive medical record review
  • 24/7 emergency support

4. Insurance Recommendations

We strongly recommend:

  • International medical insurance
  • Trip cancellation insurance
  • Medical evacuation coverage
