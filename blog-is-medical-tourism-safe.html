Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't speak Chinese?

All ISO9001-certified Grade 3A hospitals have international patient departments with English-speaking staff. Your medical coordinator provides 24/7 translation support throughout your treatment.

How are medications handled?

All medications are FDA-equivalent or original brand medications from international pharmaceutical companies. Medication lists are provided in English for your US doctor.

What about follow-up care back home?

We coordinate with your local US doctor before and after treatment. Complete medical records (in English) are provided, and our doctors are available for consultation with your US physician.
