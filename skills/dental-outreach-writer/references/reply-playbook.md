# Reply playbook

## If they ask "What exactly do you do?"
Explain in 2-3 lines:
- you support suitable patients exploring treatment abroad
- strongest fit is implant, restorative, or larger-ticket cases
- the model is referral-oriented and only for patients where cost or wait time is a real issue

## If they ask about quality
Reply with specifics, not hype:
- clinic credentials
- treatment scope
- process transparency
- patient suitability screening

## If they ask about pricing
Give ranges only if verified elsewhere. Otherwise say you can share examples after understanding the case type.

## If they show interest
Move toward:
- brief summary deck
- WhatsApp / email exchange
- short intro call

## If they are not interested
Thank them briefly and close cleanly.
