# Dental Outreach Writer

Use this skill to draft personalized English outreach messages for qualified dental leads.

## Workflow

1. Start from a qualified lead, not a cold random account.
2. Personalize using one or two observable details.
3. Keep the first touch brief and low-pressure.
4. Offer a clear collaboration angle.
5. Track what was sent in the lead sheet.

## Message rules

- Write in natural English.
- Reference something real from the account.
- Do not overclaim.
- Avoid mass-spam tone.
- Keep first contact short.
- Prefer curiosity and fit-testing over a hard sell.

## Outreach types

### 1. Public comment
Use to warm the relationship. Keep it short, specific, and respectful.

### 2. First DM
Use after qualification. Mention the creator's content angle, then introduce the collaboration idea briefly.

### 3. Follow-up DM
Use when there is no reply after a reasonable gap. Shorter than the first DM.

### 4. Reply handling
Answer questions directly. Clarify cooperation model, patient fit, and next-step call or chat.

## Collaboration angle

Frame the value around:
- patients seeking more affordable treatment options
- patients comparing implant / restorative pricing
- patients open to treatment abroad
- quality + cost-efficiency positioning
- referral-style cooperation, not vague promotion
