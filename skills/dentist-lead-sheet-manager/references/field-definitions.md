# Field definitions

## age_signal
Use broad observational tags only, such as `young-professional`, `mid-career`, or blank. Do not claim an exact age unless the profile states it.

## specialty
Examples: `general-dentist`, `cosmetic-dentist`, `implant-dentist`, `oral-surgeon`, `prosthodontist`.

## follower_count_band
Use rough bands: `0-5k`, `5k-25k`, `25k-100k`, `100k+`.

## referral_potential_score
Suggested 0-10 score based on:
- specialty fit
- content relevance
- audience engagement
- affordability/wait-time relevance
- likelihood of cross-border referral interest

## priority_level
Use `high`, `medium`, or `low`.

## notes
Keep to short factual observations, e.g. `Discusses implant pricing in Texas market; replies to patient cost questions in comments.`
