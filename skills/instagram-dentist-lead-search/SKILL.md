# Instagram Dentist Lead Search

Use this skill to search Instagram for US-based dental professionals aged roughly 25-45, then qualify them based on content, fit, and outreach potential.

## Workflow

1. Define the search target narrowly:
   - Geography: United States
   - Language: English
   - Role: dentist, dental surgeon, implant dentist, cosmetic dentist, clinic founder, oral surgeon
   - Age signal: younger personal brand, modern clinic, selfie video presence, social-first posting style
2. Search by profile keywords, hashtags, and content topics.
3. Inspect profile bio, location, website, and recent posts/reels.
4. Tag content formats and action signals.
5. Score collaboration fit.
6. Hand qualified leads to the lead sheet manager skill.

## What to capture from each account

Record:
- username
- display name
- profile URL
- bio summary
- stated location
- likely profession/specialty
- follower count band
- website/contact presence
- recent post topics
- visible patient-case content
- visible price/cost discussion
- reserve/booking/waitlist mention

## Action/form signals to tag

Mark yes/no for:
- selfie explanation
- talking to camera
- product or tool display
- teeth display / before-after reveal
- procedure demo
- surgery action display
- educational explainer
- clinic walkthrough

## Qualification guidance

Prioritize accounts that are:
- clearly US-based
- clearly English-speaking
- professionally active and still building audience
- posting educational or case-based content
- discussing implants, restorative work, veneers, or patient affordability
- likely to have patients sensitive to price or wait time

Deprioritize accounts that are:
- inactive
- meme-only or entertainment-first
- outside the US
- brands without identifiable professionals
- poor fit for referral-style collaboration

## Output

Pass qualified leads into the lead sheet using the dentist-lead-sheet-manager skill.
