# Instagram dentist search patterns

## Primary target
- US-based dental professionals
- English-speaking
- apparent age band: 25-45
- active content creators with modern personal or clinic branding

## Strong-fit specialties
- cosmetic dentist
- implant dentist
- restorative dentist
- prosthodontist
- oral surgeon
- clinic owner with visible treatment content

## Strong-fit content themes
- treatment case breakdowns
- before/after teeth transformations
- implant or veneer education
- price or financing discussion
- patient consultation explainers
- treatment planning content

## High-value profile signals
- bio names a specialty
- bio includes city/state in the US
- links to clinic booking page
- reels feature doctor speaking directly to camera
- account shows real patient cases
- comments indicate inbound patient questions

## Collaboration fit score
Score 0-2 on each:
- US-based
- English-speaking
- young-professional brand
- posts clinical cases
- posts price/cost topics
- likely referral fit

Interpretation:
- 10-12: high priority
- 7-9: medium priority
- 4-6: low priority
- 0-3: discard unless unusual strategic value
