# Instagram review checklist

## Profile-level checks
- username and display name
- role/specialty clarity
- US location present or inferable
- website or booking link present
- bio written in English

## Content-level checks
Review 3-6 recent posts/reels:
- treatment cases
- surgery or procedure clips
- price/cost discussion
- reserve/booking mention
- teeth reveal / before-after visuals
- educational selfie explanation

## Shortlist rule
A shortlisted account should have at least:
- clear dental identity
- strong US/English signal
- two or more relevant recent content signals
