# Instagram Browser Research

Use this skill to review Instagram search results and profile pages in a disciplined way.

## Workflow

1. Search by keyword and hashtag cluster.
2. Open promising profiles in separate tabs or queue.
3. Capture profile-level fields.
4. Review several recent posts/reels.
5. Tag topic and action signals.
6. Score and rank.
7. Export the top accounts.

## Search clusters

### Role clusters
- dentist
- cosmetic dentist
- implant dentist
- dental surgeon
- oral surgeon
- prosthodontist

### Topic clusters
- before and after teeth
- dental surgery
- implant cost
- veneers cost
- restorative dentistry
- smile makeover

### Intent clusters
- consultation
- booking
- reserve
- waiting list
- treatment options
- financing

## What to verify on profile pages

- US city/state signal
- English-language bio/captions
- professional identity
- clinic or booking link
- specialty focus
- whether recent content repeatedly matches the target themes

## High-value content signs

- doctor speaking directly to camera
- before/after treatment explanation
- implant or veneer case discussion
- price, financing, or treatment-option discussion
- patient questions in comments
