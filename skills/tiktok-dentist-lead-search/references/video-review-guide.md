# TikTok video review guide

## What matters most on TikTok
TikTok fit is driven more by visible behavior than by bio text alone. Review several recent videos before qualifying a lead.

## Strong-fit signs
- doctor speaks directly to camera
- clear educational framing
- treatment examples are explained simply
- visible expertise in implants, veneers, oral surgery, restorative work
- comment section contains patient questions about cost, recovery, or booking

## High-value action tags
- selfie explanation
- tool display
- teeth reveal
- procedure demonstration
- surgery motion
- consultation advice

## Fit scoring
Score 0-2 on each:
- US-based
- English-speaking
- creator energy / younger brand
- treatment-case content
- price-discussion content
- likely referral fit

Interpretation:
- 10-12: high priority
- 7-9: medium priority
- 4-6: low priority
- 0-3: discard
