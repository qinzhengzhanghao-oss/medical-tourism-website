# Tiktok Dentist Lead Search

Use this skill to find younger, camera-comfortable US dental professionals on TikTok and qualify them for outreach.

## Workflow

1. Search by niche keywords, captions, hashtags, and creator bios.
2. Review the top recent videos, not just the profile header.
3. Tag action signals from the videos.
4. Identify whether the creator appears clinically credible and commercially relevant.
5. Export qualified leads to the lead sheet.

## Search blocks

### Bio/caption keywords
- dentist
- dental surgeon
- implant dentist
- cosmetic dentist
- veneers
- implants
- smile makeover
- oral surgery
- before and after
- dental cost
- dental price

### Content themes
- treatment explanation
- surgery clip
- patient transformation
- pricing explanation
- case review
- treatment consultation
- dental experience sharing

### Hashtag seeds
- #dentistsoftiktok
- #dentaltok
- #dentistry
- #cosmeticdentist
- #dentalimplants
- #veneers
- #smilemakeover
- #teethwhitening
- #oralsurgery

## Action signals to detect

Tag yes/no for:
- selfie explanation
- talking to camera
- hand demo / action demo
- product or equipment display
- teeth close-up display
- surgery action display
- before/after reveal
- patient-case storytelling

## Qualification guidance

Prioritize creators who:
- are clearly practicing in the US
- speak English on camera or in captions
- appear younger and socially active
- explain cases, treatment choices, or pricing
- attract patient questions in comments
- show signs of patient demand, queue, or consult volume

Deprioritize creators who:
- only repost viral clips
- are not actual providers
- are agencies with no clinical authority
- have no business relevance to dental referrals

## Output

Pass qualified leads into the lead sheet using the dentist-lead-sheet-manager skill.
