# Tiktok Browser Research

Use this skill to review TikTok creators through their recent videos and profile signals.

## Workflow

1. Search by keyword and hashtag cluster.
2. Open likely creator profiles.
3. Capture profile fields.
4. Review multiple recent videos.
5. Tag visible action and topic signals.
6. Score referral fit.
7. Export the final shortlist.

## Search clusters

### Creator terms
- dentist
- dental surgeon
- implant dentist
- cosmetic dentist
- veneers
- dental implants

### Video/topic terms
- dental case
- teeth before after
- dental surgery
- implant price
- veneer price
- smile makeover
- patient consultation

### User-intent terms
- expensive dentist
- affordable dental care
- booking
- waitlist
- consultation

## What to verify

- US-based identity clues
- English-speaking captions or spoken content
- repeated dental expertise content
- younger creator style / modern branding
- evidence of patient interest in comments
