# TikTok review checklist

## Profile checks
- creator name
- specialty or dentist identity
- link in bio or clinic reference
- US location clues
- English-language presence

## Video checks
Review 4-8 recent videos:
- selfie explanation
- surgery or procedure motion
- teeth close-up or before-after
- product/tool display
- pricing discussion
- consultation/booking signals
- patient FAQ engagement in comments

## Shortlist rule
A shortlisted creator should have:
- clear provider identity
- strong dental content repeatability
- at least one strong business-fit signal such as pricing, implants, surgery, or consultation demand
