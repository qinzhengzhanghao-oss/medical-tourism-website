# Research playbook

## Goal
Turn large volumes of social browsing into a repeatable shortlist process.

## Recommended sequence
1. Run keyword and hashtag searches.
2. Open likely profiles.
3. Check bio, location, link, and specialty.
4. Review 3-6 recent posts or videos.
5. Tag content and action signals.
6. Assign score and priority.
7. Export or append to the lead sheet.

## Evidence hierarchy
Most reliable:
- bio
- location in profile
- clinic website link
- repeated treatment content across multiple posts
- comment patterns showing patient questions

Less reliable:
- a single viral post
- ambiguous hashtags
- aspirational branding with no clinical proof

## Stop conditions
Pause when:
- page requires login you do not have
- repeated captcha or suspicious activity prompts appear
- dynamic content stops loading correctly
- extracted data quality drops
