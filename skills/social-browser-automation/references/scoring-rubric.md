# Scoring rubric

Score each lead 0-2 on each dimension:
- US-based
- English-speaking
- younger professional brand
- strong dental specialty fit
- case/treatment content
- price or affordability discussion
- booking or waitlist relevance
- likely referral fit

Interpretation:
- 13-16: very high priority
- 9-12: high priority
- 5-8: medium priority
- 0-4: low priority

## Notes rule
Every score above medium should include one short evidence note.
