Safety Protocols & Standards

International safety standards, JCI-accredited hospitals, and rigorous infection control protocols for your peace of mind.

Certified Safety Standards

All our partner hospitals are JCI-accredited (Joint Commission International) and follow ISO 9001 quality management systems. We maintain the highest international safety standards.

Comprehensive Safety Protocols

Sterilization Protocol

Sterilization Standards

All dental instruments undergo Class B autoclave sterilization (134°C, 2.1 bar pressure for 18 minutes) following ADA and CDC guidelines.

  • Single-use disposable instruments
  • Class B autoclave sterilization
  • Chemical indicator monitoring
  • Biological spore testing weekly
Infection Control

Infection Control Measures

Strict infection control protocols including hand hygiene, personal protective equipment, and environmental cleaning.

  • WHO 5-moment hand hygiene
  • Full PPE for all procedures
  • Surface disinfection between patients
  • Air purification systems (HEPA filters)
Equipment Safety

Medical Equipment Safety

All medical equipment undergoes regular maintenance, calibration, and safety checks according to manufacturer specifications.

  • Regular equipment calibration
  • Preventive maintenance schedules
  • FDA/CE certified equipment only
  • Emergency backup systems

JCI Accreditation Standards

Joint Commission International (JCI) Accreditation

Gold standard for hospital quality and patient safety worldwide

✅ Patient Safety Goals

  • Correct patient identification
  • Effective communication among caregivers
  • Safe medication administration
  • Infection prevention and control

✅ Quality Standards

  • International Patient Safety Goals (IPSG)
  • Access to Care and Continuity of Care (ACC)
  • Patient and Family Rights (PFR)
  • Assessment of Patients (AOP)

JCI Accreditation Process

JCI accreditation involves rigorous on-site evaluation every 3 years, including:

  • Document review of policies and procedures
  • On-site observation of clinical practices
  • Interviews with staff and patients
  • Review of quality improvement data
