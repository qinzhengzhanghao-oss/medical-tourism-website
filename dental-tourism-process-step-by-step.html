Complete Dental Tourism Process Timeline

1

Initial Consultation & Planning (Week 1-2)

What Happens:

  • Free initial consultation via WhatsApp/Telegram
  • Dental assessment and treatment plan discussion
  • Cost estimation and package selection
  • Travel dates and accommodation planning
✅ Outcome: Clear treatment plan, confirmed dates, and complete cost breakdown
2

Travel Preparation (Week 3-4)

What Happens:

  • Visa application assistance
  • Flight booking support
  • Hotel/accommodation arrangement
  • Medical insurance setup
  • Pre-travel medical check coordination
✅ Outcome: All travel documents ready, accommodation confirmed, insurance active
3

Arrival & Treatment (Week 5-6)

What Happens:

  • Airport pickup and transfer to hotel
  • Hospital registration and final assessment
  • Dental treatment procedures
  • Daily support and translation services
  • Accommodation and meal arrangements
✅ Outcome: Successful dental treatment, comfortable stay, continuous support
4

Recovery & Follow-up (Week 7-12+)

What Happens:

  • Post-treatment recovery monitoring
  • Follow-up appointments as needed
  • Return travel arrangements
  • Long-term follow-up care plan
  • Ongoing support and consultation
✅ Outcome: Complete recovery, successful treatment outcome, ongoing care support
