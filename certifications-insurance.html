JCI Certification Process

JCI accreditation involves a rigorous 4-step process that typically takes 12-18 months to complete:

1. Application & Self-Assessment

Hospital completes detailed self-assessment against 1,300+ standards

2. Documentation Review

JCI reviews policies, procedures, and quality metrics

3. On-Site Survey

4-5 day intensive inspection by JCI surveyors

4. Accreditation Decision

Based on compliance with all critical standards
