Types of Dental Crowns Available

Zirconia Crowns

The strongest and most durable option. Zirconia crowns are metal-free, biocompatible, and provide excellent aesthetics.

Best for: Back teeth, high strength needed

E-Max Crowns

Lithium disilicate crowns offer superior aesthetics and strength. Perfect for front teeth where appearance matters most.

Best for: Front teeth, aesthetic priority

Porcelain Crowns

Traditional porcelain crowns provide good aesthetics at a lower cost. Suitable for both front and back teeth.

Best for: Cost-effective solution
