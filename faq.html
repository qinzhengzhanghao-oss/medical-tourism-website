General Questions

What is dental tourism?
Dental tourism involves traveling to another country for dental treatment, typically to access high-quality care at significantly lower costs. China has become a popular destination due to its advanced dental technology, experienced specialists, and cost savings of 60-80% compared to Western countries.
Why choose China for dental treatment?
China offers: 1) ISO9001-certified Grade 3A hospitals, 2) Experienced dental specialists with international training, 3) Advanced dental technology, 4) Cost savings of 60-80%, 5) Comprehensive service packages including travel arrangements, and 6) English-speaking support staff.
Is dental treatment in China safe?
Yes. All our partner hospitals are ISO9001-certified Grade 3A facilities, which is the highest hospital classification in China. Dentists are licensed specialists with extensive experience. We use sterilized equipment, follow international safety protocols, and provide comprehensive aftercare.

Cost & Pricing

How much can I save with dental tourism in China?
Patients typically save 60-80% compared to US prices. For example: Dental implants cost $800-$1,200 in China vs $3,000-$6,000 in the US. Full mouth restoration costs $8,000-$15,000 in China vs $30,000-$60,000 in the US. These savings include travel and accommodation costs.
What is included in the quoted price?
Our transparent pricing includes: 1) All dental procedures, 2) Hospital fees, 3) Specialist consultation, 4) Anesthesia and medications, 5) Follow-up appointments, 6) Basic travel coordination. Additional services like airport pickup, hotel accommodation, and translation are available as add-ons.
Are there any hidden costs?
No. We provide complete price transparency. You receive a detailed breakdown before committing. The only additional costs would be: 1) International flights, 2) Visa fees (if applicable), 3) Personal expenses, 4) Optional tourist activities. All medical costs are included in the quoted price.

Quality & Safety

What qualifications do your dentists have?
Our dentists are: 1) Licensed specialists with 10+ years experience, 2) Trained at top Chinese and international dental schools, 3) Members of Chinese Stomatological Association, 4) Regularly attend international dental conferences, 5) Specialized in specific areas (implants, orthodontics, etc.). You can verify credentials on our doctor profiles page.
What safety standards are followed?
We follow: 1) ISO9001 quality management standards, 2) CDC infection control guidelines, 3) Hospital-grade sterilization (autoclave), 4) Single-use instruments where applicable, 5) Digital X-rays with minimal radiation, 6) Comprehensive medical history review, 7) Allergy testing before anesthesia.
What happens if there are complications?
We provide: 1) 24/7 emergency support during your stay, 2) Free follow-up care for treatment-related issues, 3) Coordination with local medical facilities if needed, 4) Translation assistance for medical consultations, 5) Insurance claim support. Our complication rate is less than 2%, significantly lower than industry average.

Process & Timeline

What is the typical treatment timeline?
Standard timeline: 1) Consultation & planning (2-4 weeks before travel), 2) Travel to China (1-2 days), 3) Initial examination & preparation (1-2 days), 4) Main treatment procedures (3-7 days), 5) Recovery & follow-up (2-3 days), 6) Return travel. Complex cases may require multiple visits over 3-6 months.
How do I start the process?
Simple 5-step process: 1) Free online consultation (send dental records), 2) Receive treatment plan & quote, 3) Schedule dates & make travel arrangements, 4) Travel to China for treatment, 5) Follow-up care & support. We handle all logistics - you just need to show up for appointments.
Do I need a visa for China?
Most nationalities require a tourist visa (L visa). We provide: 1) Invitation letter from hospital, 2) Guidance on visa application, 3) Document checklist, 4) Support for medical visa if needed. Processing typically takes 4-10 business days. Some countries qualify for visa-free transit (72/144 hours).
