⚠️ Important Safety Information & Patient Responsibilities

Medical Considerations

  • Dental implants require adequate bone density and healthy gums
  • Not suitable for uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smokers, or certain medical conditions
  • Success rates typically 95-98%, but individual results vary
  • Healing time 3-6 months before final crowns can be placed
  • Possible complications: infection, nerve damage, implant failure

Patient Responsibilities

  • Provide complete and accurate medical history
  • Follow all pre- and post-operative instructions
  • Attend all scheduled appointments and follow-ups
  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene during healing
  • Report any complications or concerns immediately
