Common Patient Concerns & Our Solutions

Concern #1: "Is the quality of medical care in China comparable to Western standards?"

Patients worry about medical standards, equipment quality, and doctor qualifications.

Our Solution:

  • ISO9001 quality system certification: All partner hospitals are Joint Commission International certified (same standard as US hospitals)
  • Seasoned dental experts: Medical staff trained at Harvard, Johns Hopkins, Mayo Clinic
  • Same Equipment: GE, Siemens, Philips medical equipment identical to US hospitals
  • 98.2% Success Rate: Documented treatment outcomes matching US standards

Concern #2: "What if something goes wrong during treatment?"

Fear of complications, lack of follow-up care, and communication barriers.

Our Solution:

  • Complication Insurance: $500,000 medical complication coverage included
  • 24/7 English Support: Dedicated case manager available round-the-clock
  • Follow-up Guarantee: 12 months post-treatment follow-up included
  • Emergency Protocol: Immediate transfer to international hospital if needed

Concern #3: "How do I know the prices are transparent with no hidden fees?"

Worry about unexpected costs, hidden charges, and price manipulation.

Our Solution:

  • All-Inclusive Pricing: Treatment, hospital stay, medications, follow-up included
  • Price Lock Guarantee: Quoted price is final, no hidden fees
  • Itemized Breakdown: Detailed cost breakdown before payment
  • Money-Back Guarantee: Full refund if prices change after quote

Concern #4: "What about language barriers and cultural differences?"

Communication challenges, cultural misunderstandings, and feeling isolated.

Our Solution:

  • Dedicated Translator: Medical translator assigned for entire stay
  • Cultural Liaison: Helps with customs, food preferences, local etiquette
  • English-Speaking Staff: All hospital staff speak fluent English
  • Patient Community: Connect with previous international patients
